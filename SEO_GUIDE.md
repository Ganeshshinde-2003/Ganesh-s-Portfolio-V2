## ✅ Completed SEO Improvements

### 1. **Enhanced Metadata in Layout (app/layout.tsx)**
   - **Title Tag**: "Ganesh Shinde | AI Product Engineer & Full-Stack Developer"
   - **Meta Description**: Comprehensive description including all key skills
   - **Keywords**: 30+ keywords targeting different search intents
   - **Open Graph Tags**: For better social media sharing
   - **Twitter Card Tags**: For Twitter/X previews
   - **Canonical URL**: Prevents duplicate content issues

### 2. **Structured Data (JSON-LD)**
   - **Person Schema**: Tells Google who you are, your job title, location, email, social profiles
   - **Website Schema**: Describes your site purpose and structure
   - **Benefits**: Better understanding by Google, potentially rich snippets in search results

### 3. **Sitemap (public/sitemap.xml)**
   - Lists all important pages and sections
   - Helps Google discover and crawl all content
   - Includes priority and update frequency for each page

### 4. **Robots.txt (public/robots.txt)**
   - Allows search engines to crawl your site
   - Prevents indexing of sensitive files
   - Specifies sitemap location
   - Handles AI crawler preferences

### 5. **SEO Configuration File (lib/seoConfig.ts)**
   - Centralized keyword management
   - 80+ keywords covering all your skills and projects
   - Organized by category (primary, technical, AI/ML, domain-specific, etc.)
